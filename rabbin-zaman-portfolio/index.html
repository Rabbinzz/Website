II THE NARRATIVE ARC

Curious first, strategic always

Every field trains a certain kind of attention. Marketing trained mine on people — what they want before they can name it, and why they choose one story over another.

I'm an undergraduate student reading Marketing, and somewhere between the coursework and the case studies I picked up a genuine appetite for business, strategy, and entrepreneurship — not as subjects to pass, but as a way of seeing.

Dhaka is loud, fast, and unsentimental about ideas that don't hold up. It's a good place to learn that growth is a discipline, not a mood.

III THE EXECUTION & PROOF

Ideas earn their keep on delivery

Strategy is easy to talk about and hard to ship. My approach is to treat every assignment, pitch, or side project the same way a working marketer would — define the problem precisely, test the smallest version of the idea, then scale what actually holds up.

This chapter is where the coursework, case competitions, and self-directed projects will live as they're finished — a running record of things built, not just planned.
